Technical Architecture & Engineering

Built natively from scratch following modern Android development best practices.

Jetpack Compose & Material 3

Modern declarative UI toolkit featuring adaptive layout support, edge-to-edge rendering, and Material You dynamic color harmonizing.

WallpaperService & RenderEffect

High-performance Android Live Wallpaper service utilizing GPU hardware acceleration and RenderEffect shader blur pipelines.

MediaSessionObserver

Low-overhead background observer that hooks into active media sessions (Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music) with zero battery drain.

AndroidX Palette Engine

Asynchronous multi-swatch extraction extracting dominant, vibrant, muted, light vibrant, and dark muted tones to skin widgets on the fly.
