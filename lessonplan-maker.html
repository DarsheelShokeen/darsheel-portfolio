LessonPlan Maker: Curriculum pedagogy workbench

Educational platform generating lesson plans aligned with NEP 2020 inquiry pedagogy.

Role Full Stack Web Architect
Core technologies Python, Flask, Jinja2, NEP 2020
Status Deployed and operational
Scope Middle school classes 6 to 8

1. Platform overview

LessonPlan Maker simplifies curriculum planning for middle school educators. It translates pedagogical requirements into structured lesson units conforming to the National Education Policy 2020.

Educators configure subjects, learning goals, and Bloom taxonomy stages, while the platform synthesizes inquiry activities, assessments, and teacher guides.

2. Inquiry model integration

The engine models five structured stages of inquiry learning:

Engage: Generates starter prompts and real-world hooks to capture student curiosity.

Explore and explain: Structures collaborative lab tasks and concept clarifications.

Elaborate and evaluate: Provides application scenarios and formative rubrics.

3. Architecture and output pipeline

Built using a Python Flask backend with modular schema validation. The engine exports structured HTML print views and clean JSON payloads for school management systems.
