What I'm doing right now.

This page acts as a public declaration of my active priorities, learning trajectories, and explorations. Inspired by Derek Sivers' /now page movement.

Location: Delhi, India • Last updated: October 2026
Building
  • AlbumCanvas: Expanding lock screen wallpaper rendering pipelines and experimenting with dynamic mesh shaders.
  • LessonPlan Maker: Polishing the 5E inquiry sequencing module for NCERT middle school curriculum and print styling.
  • Financial Research Platform: Developing corporate financial statement normalization schemas and interactive DCF models.
Learning
  • Android IPC & SurfaceFlinger: Understanding how low-level window buffers and wallpaper services interact with the Android composition pipeline.
  • Capital Allocation & Valuation: Studying Damodaran's cost of capital frameworks and return on invested capital (ROIC) across Indian industries.
  • Embedded RTOS: FreeRTOS task scheduling and sleep mode power conservation on ESP32 microcontrollers.
Exploring
  • Swiss Editorial Typography: Studying negative space, grid systems, and high-contrast editorial hierarchy in digital publications.
  • Offline-First Systems: Architecture for zero-dependency client-side pedagogical engines operating in school environments with low connectivity.
  • Micro-Sensors in Smart Cities: Evaluating resilient enclosure designs and capacitive vs. resistive moisture probes for flood alert hardware.
Researching
  • Equity Valuation Discrepancies: How working capital variations distort reported free cash flow metrics in consumer and manufacturing firms.
  • NEP 2020 Pedagogical Frameworks: Structuring middle-stage competency outcomes into computable web state models.
  • Decentralized Telemetry: Lightweight protocols for environmental sensors over constrained 4G networks.
