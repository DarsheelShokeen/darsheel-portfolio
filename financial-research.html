Financial Research Platform — Systematic Valuation & Statement Analytics

An ongoing research and engineering initiative exploring automated corporate financial statement parsing, normalized ratio computation, and interactive Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation models.

Role Financial Analyst & Systems Engineer
Core Technologies Python, Pandas, Valuation Mathematics, REST API Schema, SQLite
Status In Active Development (Research & Engine Prototyping)
Focus Area Equity Valuation & Capital Structure

1. Project Introduction

Investment research often involves extracting data from dense, idiosyncratic PDF annual reports and quarterly filing disclosures. Retail investors and independent researchers either spend hours entering numbers into static spreadsheets or rely on black-box commercial terminals with steep recurring costs.

The Financial Research Platform is designed to bridge this divide. It creates a standardized data pipeline that ingests raw balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements, standardizes them across reporting periods, and feeds them into systematic valuation engines.

2. The Problem Being Addressed

  • Inconsistent Accounting Nomenclature: Line items like "Other Operating Expenses" or "Share-Based Compensation" vary across accounting regimes (Ind-AS vs. US GAAP vs. IFRS), causing distorted automated screeners.
  • Static, Rigid Financial Models: Traditional Excel spreadsheets lack sensitivity analysis interfaces and require manual formula reconstruction when historical restatements occur.
  • Neglect of Capital Structure Nuance: Simple P/E or EV/EBITDA screeners overlook lease liabilities, non-controlling interests, and dilution from convertible instruments.

3. Project Objectives & Mathematical Rigor

"Valuation is neither pure science nor pure art; it is disciplined craft rooted in cash flow reality."

The platform's valuation methodology is built on explicit, verifiable financial principles:

  • Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF): Unlevered cash flows defined as \( \text{NOPAT} + \text{D\&A} - \Delta \text{NWC} - \text{CapEx} \).
  • Dynamic Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC): Calibrating cost of equity via CAPM using realistic country risk premiums and levered betas.
  • Two-Stage Horizon Modeling: High-growth projection period transitioning smoothly to a sustainable terminal growth rate capped at long-term GDP growth.
